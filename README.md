## Get started

1. **[Download the latest installer](https://github.com/JPEG111/orbs-release/releases/latest)** and install Orbs. No Python required.
2. **Open Discord**, then choose your Discord client and language in Orbs setup.
3. **Click Scan & start** on Home or in Library.

Scan & start accepts supported desktop play quests, selects Desktop, builds a
queue using the remaining quest time, and starts it. Keep Discord's interface
in **English** while scanning, and leave its window open without navigating or scrolling.

Want manual control? Turn off **One-click scan & start** in Settings. Use
**Library → Discover** to find games, then adjust times and order in **Queue**.

## Make it yours

- **English / العربية** — choose at first launch or change it in Settings.
- **Automatic mode** — optional launch, scan and queue start at Windows sign-in,
  with a startup delay and cancellation control.
- **Editable queues** — change play times and order. Completed games advance;
  interrupted games keep their remaining time for retry.
- **Local progress** — estimated Orbs, time saved and an animated rank ladder.

All setup choices remain available in **Settings**.

## Updates & saved data

Orbs checks for updates when opened and every six hours. Installation waits for
active queues and scans to finish; Windows may ask for administrator approval.
You can also use **Settings → Check for updates**.

Updates and reinstalls keep your saved queue and preferences. Closing the window
keeps Orbs running in the system tray; use the tray menu to quit.

## Before you use it

Game support and quest credit are **not guaranteed**. Check progress in Discord.
Videos, in-Discord activities and reward claiming are not automated. Ambiguous
or unsupported quests stay available for review.

Simulated activity may violate Discord's rules and put your account at risk.
Orbs is independent of Discord and provided without warranty. It does not use
your account token or patch the Discord client.

## Need help?

If a scan fails, check the selected client and English Discord interface, then
open **All Quests** and retry. Diagnostics are available in **Settings → Open logs**.
Scanning waits through common loading states and can leave recognized settings;
finish any unsaved Discord edits first. The cursor may stay still during a scan.
The installer is unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
