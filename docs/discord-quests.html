Discord Quest types: what requires real play?

Discord offers different Quest objectives. Seeing a game title doesn't always mean the task is a supported desktop play session. Always read the specific Quest instructions shown in Discord.

Policy matters.

Discord prohibits scripts or spoofed apps that simulate Quest completion without the required activity. Orbs cannot make simulation authorized or undetectable. For a compliant approach, complete the task in the game, stream, video, or Discord activity described by Discord. Official policy.

Why a scan may find a Quest but not queue it

Orbs reads the currently loaded Quests interface; it isn't a direct account Quest API. A visible title alone doesn't establish the supported desktop game identity, eligible-games membership, task readiness, or remaining time. Missing or conflicting information stays review-only rather than being silently treated as playable.

What a queue timer actually means

The timer tracks local Orbs playback duration; it is not a measured Discord heartbeat or a verified reward balance. If Discord rejects a local Rich Presence update or a worker stops unexpectedly, Orbs may pause that queue item for manual retry. Even a completed timer does not establish Quest credit. This distinction is important when troubleshooting a Quest that appears stuck.
